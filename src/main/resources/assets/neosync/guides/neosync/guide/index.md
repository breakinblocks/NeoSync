# <Color id="gold">NeoSync</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="sync_core" scale="2"/>
  One mind. Many bodies.
</Column>

NeoSync lets you grow spare bodies, called shells, and move your mind between them. Every shell keeps its own inventory, experience and game mode. Leave one body in storage on the other side of the world, sync into it whenever you like, and sync back when you are done.

***
## <Color id="gold">The Loop</Color>

<Row>
<Column>

### <Color id="aqua">1. Build</Color>

Craft a <ItemLink id="sync_core"/>, then a <ItemLink id="shell_constructor"/>. Right-click the constructor with an empty hand to give it a genetic sample and start growing a shell.

</Column>
<Column>

### <Color id="aqua">2. Power</Color>

Put a <ItemLink id="treadmill"/> against the constructor and lure a pig or wolf onto it. Any FE source works too.

</Column>
</Row>

<Row>
<Column>

### <Color id="aqua">3. Store</Color>

Craft a <ItemLink id="shell_storage"/>, place it and power it with redstone or FE. The doors open when it has power.

</Column>
<Column>

### <Color id="aqua">4. Sync</Color>

Walk into the open storage. A radial menu shows every shell you own. Pick one and your mind moves into it.

</Column>
</Row>
