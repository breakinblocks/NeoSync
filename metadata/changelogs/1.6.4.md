# NeoSync 1.6.4

Getting into a Shell Storage or Shell Constructor built on a physics object no longer takes a jump or a crouch. The doorway now clears your whole body and there is no step up into the block.

Syncing back into a body that sits on a physics object plays cleanly. The flight back used a fixed point in the world, so a moving ship left the camera stranded in mid air until it snapped you back into place. Those syncs now switch you straight over instead.

Standing in a Shell Storage on a physics object no longer makes the shell selector open again on its own, and you get pushed back out of the block when you leave it, the same as on the ground.
