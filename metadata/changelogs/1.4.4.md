# NeoSync 1.4.4

Shells made before 1.4.0 load again. They were saved without the details that airship support later added, and a world carried forward from an older version would turn players away at the door with "Invalid player data" and fail to load shell storages and constructors placed in the world. Anything an older shell was saved without now falls back to a sensible value, and the shell is written out complete the next time it saves.
