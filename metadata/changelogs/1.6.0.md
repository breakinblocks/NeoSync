# NeoSync 1.6.0

Radiation from Mekanism now belongs to the body instead of following you around. Sync into a fresh shell and you wake up clean. The body you leave behind in a shell storage keeps whatever dose it picked up, so syncing back into it gives you the radiation back rather than letting you wash it off by body hopping. Shells that come out of the constructor and bodies you respawn into from an anchor both start clean.

This only applies when Mekanism is installed. Nothing changes without it.
