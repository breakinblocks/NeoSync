# NeoSync 1.4.2

Picking a shell away from a shell storage works now. There is nowhere to leave your body when you are out in the world, so you die on the spot and wake up in the shell you picked once you respawn. Grave mods keep your items and xp through it, and without one you get the usual death drop. You can also pick a shell while you are already dead and you will arrive there instead of at your spawn point when you respawn, so a single use anchor really does cover one death.

Dying in a shell no longer eats what you were carrying on worlds that have keep inventory turned on. Your items drop where you died like they always should have.
